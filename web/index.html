How it works

ToolPain is the harness. Vultr is the substrate. An agentic loop on Vultr Inference (Qwen) reads the repo, writes bootstrap and probe programs, and the scanner runs them in isolation.

  1. 01Provision on VultrControl plane creates (or claims) an ephemeral scanner VM from the baked snapshot.
  2. 02Agent codegenQwen on Vultr Inference reviews the repo and emits setup, launch, and probe programs under guards.
  3. 03Isolate & probeDocker sandbox, sinkholed network, decoys planted. Every tool is called and traced.
  4. 04Analyze & certifyClaims vs runtime, privilege, egress. LLM writes IT-facing findings and the certificate.
  5. 05DestroyReport saved on the control plane. Scanner VM destroyed. Nothing persists on the host.
